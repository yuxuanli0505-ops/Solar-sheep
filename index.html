Model Assumptions

Research notes
  • Solar output is modeled as a monthly clean-energy estimate affected by dust loss and cleaning frequency.
  • Vegetation cover is used as a proxy indicator for grass recovery and NDVI-style ecological restoration.
  • Sheep carrying capacity is estimated from vegetation cover and compared with livestock demand.
  • CO2 avoided is estimated from clean electricity replacing grid electricity.
  • Verification is represented as a data transparency and integrity layer; future versions can add dataset hashes and timestamps.
